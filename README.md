I earned my bachelor's degree in Data Science at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), where I am now pursuing a master's degree in Computer Science.

## Undergraduate thesis

I built a collaborative speculative decoding system for large language models across an Android phone and a Windows PC. The phone runs Qwen2.5-0.5B to propose tokens, and the PC runs Qwen2.5-7B to verify them.

In a local-network experiment generating about 1,000 tokens, the system sustained **14.0 tokens/s**. That was **93.5% higher** than running the 7B model on the phone alone and **94.5% of the throughput** of two-process speculative decoding running entirely on the Windows PC.
